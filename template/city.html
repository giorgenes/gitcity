A building's height is proportional to the file's size at the drawn commit, however many commits it has. Moving through history stacks the blocks up to that height; it does not replay past sizes. 1 block = 1 commit: its width and its share of the building follow the commit's total lines. A hunk's min(deleted, added) lines count as modified, and a modified line is both a delete and an addition. The largest tenth of files are dense: a tenth of that height, with their extension written around them.
