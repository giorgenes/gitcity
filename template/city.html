A building's height is proportional to the file's size at the drawn commit, however many commits it has. Moving through history stacks the blocks up to that height; it does not replay past sizes. 1 block = 1 commit: its width and its share of the building follow the commit's total lines. A hunk's min(deleted, added) lines count as modified, and a modified line is both a delete and an addition. A file too tall for the height limit is divided, by the smallest of 10, 100 or 1000 that fits. It is drawn darker at each level and marked around its walls: d = 10x, c = 100x, k = 1000x.
